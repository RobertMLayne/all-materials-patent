Both vectors are positive and normalized. Primitive target counts are (Ti,O) = (4,7), with total 11, or any positive integer multiple for accounting compatibility. With delta = 1/4 and an expressly assumed 2N_Ti oxygen-site reference, the missing-site fraction is 1/8, the occupied-reference-site fraction is 7/8, and the occupied-atom oxygen fraction is 7/11. These are different denominators. Vacancies are not atoms. The finite count calculation does not establish a rutile vacancy map, exact specimen inventory or phase stability.

Under the restricted neutral O(2-) and Ti(III)/Ti(IV)-only formal-charge assumptions, half of Ti atoms are assigned each formal valence; each population is 2/11 of all ideal host atoms. The ideal charge sum is 3*2 + 4*2 - 2*7 = 0. These are CALCULATED populations, not measured oxidation states or carriers, and they do not increase k. A mass-basis conversion would instead use 4*M_Ti/(4*M_Ti+7*M_O) and its oxygen complement, with positive finite isotope-qualified effective masses and a stated allocation convention.
