## Governing comparison framework and current official sources

A limitation chart asks whether one qualifying reference supplies every actual limitation as arranged, including justified necessary inherency. An enabled species can defeat a broader genus, while an enormous generic menu does not automatically disclose every selected physical species. Specific points, broad ranges and bounded combinations need their own factual analysis. [USPTO anticipation, genus/species and range guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html)

A possible property is insufficient for inherency. Reference enablement can draw on relevant skilled knowledge and does not categorically require performed author experiments. It differs from the applicant's full-scope enablement obligation. [USPTO inherency guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2112.html), [reference operability guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html)

Obviousness review identifies the actual difference, relevant teachings, supported modification/selection rationale, reasonable expectation and contrary evidence. Range overlap or optimization must be grounded in the system and art; catalog membership alone does not supply that reasoning. [USPTO section 103 guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2143.html), [range and species-selection guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2144.html)

Textual correspondence is not a complete legal written-description determination. The physical disclosure must be commensurate with the claim's scope, and prophetic work must remain identifiable as unperformed. Broad scope alone is not indefiniteness; unclear boundaries require a separate inquiry. [USPTO written-description guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2163.html), [enablement guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2164.html), [definiteness guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2173.html)

For record-method eligibility, the current framework includes the MPEP and the immediately effective December 2025 Desjardins update. A described technological improvement must be reflected in the claimed ordered combination; labels or usefulness alone do not certify eligibility. The current claims recite no particular computer or operative fabrication/control step. This observation identifies a review issue rather than a final rejection. [USPTO eligibility guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2106.html), [Desjardins memorandum](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/memo-desjardins.pdf)

The **29 September 2026 SMED memorandum supplements April 2026**, retains optional declarations and announces no new practice. April superseded the December 2025 applicant/practitioner best-practices memo; the separate December examiner memo remains operative. [September 2026 memorandum](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/smeds-memo-20260929.pdf), [April 2026 memorandum](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/smeds-application-practitioners-4302026.pdf)

The accompanying flowchart separates formalities from merits, examines objective evidence and its connection to the claim, weighs the whole record and prevents evidence from supplying disclosure required originally. No executed SMED or new factual technical-improvement declaration is prepared here. [Official SMED flowchart](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/smeds-flowchart.pdf)

Ordinary human conception standards apply with or without AI. The November 2025 guidance rescinded the February 2024 AI-specific guidance; AI remains a tool. Proposed naming, source attribution and actual conception must therefore remain distinct. [Revised USPTO inventorship guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2025/revised-inventorship-guidance-ai-assisted-inventions)

Unity needs a common special technical contribution over the art. The current WIPO ISPE edition is PCT/GL/ISPE/14, effective 1 January 2026; common subject matter can lose its linking contribution after prior-art review. A catalog, dependency graph or common denominator is not a unity certificate. [PCT Rule 13](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/rules/r13), [current ISPE guidelines](https://www.wipo.int/documents/d/pct-system/docs-en-texts-ispe.pdf)

A later PCT must make its description, claims and drawings sufficiently clear for a meaningful search. Article 17 permits a declaration that no search report will be established where prescribed requirements prevent such a search; it also provides for additional fees when unity is lacking. Paying fees does not supply missing technical teaching. These are potential examination issues for the broad candidate bank, not assessed fees or a declaration already issued for this draft. [PCT Article 17](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/articles/a17)

These official sources describe examination practice and applicable statutory inquiries. This guide does not certify every jurisdiction's law, examination outcome or worldwide patent coverage.

A provisional benefit claim requires support for the later claimed subject matter plus procedural entitlement. Optional broad claims do not themselves confer priority over every species. Necessary relied-on content must be present in accepted application contents; a general repository link is not an assumed substitute. A PCT application requires its own contents and examination path, and international search/preliminary examination does not grant worldwide patent rights. [USPTO earlier-benefit guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s211.html), [PCT application contents](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/articles/a3)

For the defensive-publication objective, a separately authorized later PCT should consider an express early-publication request. Ordinary international publication occurs promptly after 18 months from the priority date; Article 21 permits an earlier applicant request. Filing this review document alone is not immediate public publication. [PCT Article 21](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/articles/a21)

Public availability and an effective U.S. patent-document prior-art date are different inquiries. Under the AIA, a qualifying WIPO-published PCT designating the United States can be prior art under section 102(a)(2) as effectively filed for the relied-on subject matter, subject to the other-inventor requirement and applicable exceptions. English publication and U.S. national-stage entry are not prerequisites under that AIA rule. No such filing, publication or effective date is assigned here. [USPTO section 2154 guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2154.html)
