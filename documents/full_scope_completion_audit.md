# Full-scope objective audit

Prepared 30 September 2026 UTC. This audit preserves the user's full requested outcome: a comprehensive provisional/PCT materials disclosure spanning elemental supports and proportion ranges through 138 labels, isotopes and known/hypothetical particles, expected and contrary properties, and an intended bar to all or nearly all later materials patents. It does not redefine that outcome as successful numerical enumeration or document preparation.

The original working draft has 80 numbered paragraphs and 199 candidate claims. The extension adds an [unrestricted-domain and physical-accounting supplement](technical_scope_supplement.md), [specific literature records](reference_entry_support.md), dated [nuclear](../data/nuclear_archive/README.md) and [particle](../data/particle_archive/README.md) identity archives, and a [current publication-status addendum](current_status_addendum.md). Original claims, paragraph numbering and PDFs are retained. There is no official filing receipt or claimed applicant experiment in this package.

## Evidence present, and what it proves

The [unrestricted model](../range_model/unrestricted_composition_model.json), [examples](../range_model/unrestricted_numeric_examples.json), [implementation](../range_model/unrestricted_compositions.py) and [recorded report](../range_model/unrestricted_verification_report.json) use exact fractions without a chosen positive floor. All support cardinalities are checked mathematically. Closed-box/open-simplex feasibility and arbitrary-denominator count feasibility are distinct. A fixed N-atom realization requires the primitive elemental denominator to divide N; isotope/state partitions require each joint sub-count to be integral as well.

The implementation report records 11 bounded groups. Separate independent checks passed 1,812 positive-box cases, 2,400 lattice boxes, 14,609 primitive-vector/inventory cases, 1,805 joint-partition cases, eight invalid-partition cases and 65 explicit uniform-subset cases. Those oracles use vertices, separator positions, independent common-denominator counts and explicit subsets. They support the algorithms and stated proofs. They do not experimentally test the indexed materials or exhaust an infinite domain.

The nuclear verifier accounts for every preserved source row and reconstructs raw columns; its deliberate-defect cases reject lost rows, altered identities/status and misread bounds. The portable package can reconstruct the optional normalized table in memory without a new network download. This proves fidelity to one source file, not the existence or availability of every listed state. The paper's 30 October 2020 availability statement, the ASCII server modification date, its literal 2021 discovery-year field and the retrieval date retain separate meanings.

The particle verifier reports all selected source rows/columns match the pinned PDG SQLite source. Original nulls, aliases, search groups and ambiguous duplicate names remain. The export contains 1,170 charge-state identity rows and complete declared supporting selections, rather than all measurements or the entire publication. [PDG's API scope and limitations](https://pdgapi.lbl.gov/doc/status.html) remain applicable. Neither the `PART` code nor the database release status is a discovery assessment.

The [reference entries](reference_entry_support.md) develop separately identified records across all five material families in the blueprint. They supply particular source-backed preparation/characterization details, quantity-basis conversions and conditional comparisons, and explicitly retain missing reproduction fields. The new molecular/polymer/composite records are mapped to the relevant original claim fields without treating them as evidence for claim 1's exact-zero whole-product inventory. They remain attributed prior publications; adding them to this package does not create an applicant experiment, newly conceived invention or universal preparation rule.

## Technical fields still missing over the requested physical domain

| Material class | Important unresolved fields for a selected candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Metals/alloys | Actual feed purity and charge, phase relations, atmosphere, volatility/immiscibility, consolidation, thermal/mechanical history, specimen assay and property protocol. |
| Inorganic compounds/ceramics | Precursors, charge/site and defect constraints, reaction path, phases/polymorphs, crystallization/conditioning, isolation, purity and structure confirmation. |
| Molecular materials | Connectivity, stereochemistry, counterions/solvation, reaction or assembly, purification and chemical identity; overall element percentages do not supply a molecule. |
| Polymers | Repeat units, incorporated composition, sequence/tacticity, distributions, end groups, branching/crosslinks, preparation and conditioning. |
| Composites/mixtures | Constituent identities, geometry, interfaces, dispersion, phase/void fractions, assembly, local/bulk denominator and property direction. |
| Nuclear/particle systems | Actual species/state, existence evidence, production, energy/occupation convention, host/confinement, interactions, lifetime, decay inventory, observation and a physically meaningful system/use. |

Across all classes, identify sample size/region, measurement uncertainty, detection/quantification capability, impurities and unknown residuals. A zero in a mathematical target is not a measured absence. A nominal host or principal-element ratio is not automatically a closed whole-product inventory. Charge, site occupancy, stability, processing history and isotope partitioning must not be defaulted from a normalization witness.

Property work needs a baseline, observable, conditions, comparison threshold, supported model or actual data and a credible alternative mechanism. Increased/decreased/unchanged categories leave infinitely many magnitudes, transitions, anisotropies, histories and uses unspecified. A source-supported response for one material family is not evidence for every sixth through 138th constituent or hypothetical nuclide.

## Legal and filing requirements still missing

The intended prior-art effect requires claim-by-claim comparisons of actual teachings, support, applicable dates and statutory exceptions. [USPTO novelty guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html) does not make generic menus automatically disclose every species or narrower range. [Prior-art enablement guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html) distinguishes teaching how the subject matter can be carried out from merely naming it. Experiments are not categorically required for every example, and absence of applicant experiments alone is not the decisive gap; missing technical direction across this physical scope is.

Record the actual human technical contribution and inventors, applicant/entitlement information, relevant disclosure/filing history, jurisdictional requirements, fee eligibility and chosen receiving Office/search authority. No values are invented. Prepare and verify the accepted submitted description, claims, drawings, abstract and relied-on annexes. [PCT content guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/ispe/4_02_27) and [Rule 4.9](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/rules/r4) govern essential disclosure/designations; repository links and general incorporation language are not filing receipts or a cure for absent support. Unity, meaningful-search and national/regional requirements remain separate assessments.

The original publication observation establishes an observed public version by its stated observation time, not a backdated preparation date. Any final extension publication must identify its own exact contents and public-access evidence. A future provisional does not automatically restore a universally prepublication position; the actual disclosure chronology and jurisdiction-specific consequences need review. No successful filing, fee payment or certification is reported here.
