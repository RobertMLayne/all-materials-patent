# Applicant evidence intake

Prepared 30 September 2026. This blank working template connects TECH-001, EXP-001 through EXP-003, and PAT-001 to the existing [pending-work record](../AGENTS.md#pending-work-record). It does not identify an applicant invention, assign inventorship, report an experiment, or certify filing readiness. Every response is **UNPROVIDED** until supported information is supplied; an unanswered field is not a negative finding or a default value.

Use one private working copy per candidate contribution. Keep personal applicant details, unpublished technical evidence, private file locations and disclosure records outside this public repository until their inclusion is deliberately reviewed. Do not enter credentials, access tokens, identity-document numbers or payment details. Refer to private evidence by a non-sensitive record identifier in any public version.

The [blueprint](pct_defensive_disclosure_blueprint.md#disclosure-entry-architecture), [five material reference families](reference_entry_support.md), [technical supplement](technical_scope_supplement.md), and [full-scope audit](full_scope_completion_audit.md) already supply the organizing framework. This intake asks for the missing evidence rather than repeating it. ESTABLISHED, CALCULATED, PROPOSED and UNSUPPORTED retain their meanings in those documents. Literature teachings do not become applicant contributions through transcription; a proposed study remains unperformed in this package.
