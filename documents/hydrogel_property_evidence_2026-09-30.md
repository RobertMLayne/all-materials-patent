## Reported material, preparation and measurement

The study uses azide-functionalized polyisocyanide (PIC) 1 and bifunctional linker 2a. Cold aqueous polymer/linker solutions are mixed, then heated into the gel phase for a one-hour crosslinking period. Figure 4 uses PIC 1 mg/mL, crosslinking at 37 °C and 11 hours of stabilization at 5 °C; the DBCO/azide functional-group ratio spans 0–2. Each linker supplies two DBCO groups. Rheometry specifies 40 mm sandblasted parallel plates, 500 µm gap, strain amplitude 0.04, frequency 1 Hz and a 1 °C/min temperature ramp.

Supplementary page 1 gives polymerization from purified methoxy-terminated monomer and azide-functionalized monomer with a nickel catalyst, 72 hours at room temperature, repeated precipitation and a reported 244 mg product. This source recipe and its precursor references must be consulted together; this paragraph is not a complete synthesis instruction or a whole-product elemental assay.

## Measurement, model and extrapolation remain separate

The authors estimate active-crosslink concentration using measured conversion and a two-reaction assumption. They explain poor stabilization at excess linker by singly attached linker rather than effective bridges. This is a model-supported interpretation, not a direct count of every active bridge. The authors describe the near-stoichiometric optimum as expected.

For the disclosure architecture, classify the reported preparation and moduli as ESTABLISHED attributed source teachings; identify estimated active crosslinks as model-derived and retain their assumptions. A nominal functional-group ratio is neither an elemental atomic/mass fraction nor a measured bridge fraction. The numerical composition generators supply none of those substitutions.
