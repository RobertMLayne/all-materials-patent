## What comprehensive mathematical coverage means

For a selected support S with k labels, use fractions x_i > 0 for labels actually in S, x_j = 0 outside S, and sum(x_i) = 1. The union over supports represents zero-coordinate boundaries through smaller supports. A 100% single-label coordinate is a unary endpoint; a two-or-more-label support has each selected coordinate below 100%. Percentages equal 100x_i. The 138-label vocabulary retains 118 recognized elements and 20 hypothetical placeholders; names do not establish availability or existence.

For each support and explicitly declared composition basis, define correlated intervals l_i <= x_i <= u_i subject to normalization and positivity. Arbitrarily fine rational endpoints can be addressed without a common denominator cap or a minimum positive rational concentration. A requested fractional interval must also be distinguished from an interval expressed in percent. An infeasible collection of independently chosen component intervals is not a material composition merely because every individual bound lies between zero and one.

The [unrestricted model](../range_model/unrestricted_composition_model.json) and [technical supplement](technical_scope_supplement.md) already make these distinctions. Their all-rational domain is broader than the historical denominator-10^19 grid. Symbolic real targets include irrational coordinates, which are not an exhaustively enumerable rational table. A finite exported sample is not the entire infinite domain. Refining an interval does not remove its narrower subintervals from the mathematical domain; this set-membership observation does not establish their individual disclosure for patent purposes.

For a finite retained atomic inventory with N atoms, x_i = n_i/N and a one-atom inventory increment is 1/N, or 100/N percent. N is specimen-dependent; this equation supplies neither a universal smallest fraction nor an assay limit. Exact mass fractions use a different denominator and depend on the applicable masses. Atomic, mass, molar, feed, repeat-unit, site and loading bases must not be silently interchanged.

No universal “most measurably possible” interval is assigned. The measurand, material matrix, analyte, sample, procedure, calibration and uncertainty must be specified for an actual measurement claim. A coordinate with many decimal places is not evidence that it can be prepared or resolved. This application of measurement principles is consistent with [NIST TN 1297, Appendix D.4](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-d4-measurand-defined-measurement-method); NIST does not supply a universal composition cutoff.

## Why containing every range does not establish a universal patent bar

Anticipation compares every later claim limitation with a qualifying reference. A disclosed species can anticipate an encompassing genus. The reverse is not automatic: broad genera, large combination menus and overlapping ranges require sufficient specificity in context. A specifically taught point inside a later range may matter, but mathematical containment alone does not decide novelty. [USPTO MPEP §§2131.02–.03](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html).

Overlapping ranges or routine optimization can support an obviousness case, with a reasoned rationale and reasonable expectation of success. Criticality, unexpected results and teaching away can affect that case. Labeling every possible interval does not establish those findings for every later selection. [USPTO MPEP §§2144.05 and 2144.08](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2144.html).

Absence of applicant experiments is not categorically fatal. Relevant literature, valid theory and skilled-person knowledge can supply an enabling teaching. Conversely, naming a composition does not supply an unavailable preparation route. A reference may contribute to obviousness even when it is not itself enabling; the prior art as a whole must enable the particular claimed invention, and its application requires a supported rationale. [USPTO MPEP §§2121–2121.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html), [Raytheon, official opinion pp. 10–11](https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/20-1755.opinion.4-16-2021_1764430.pdf).

The scope of an applicant's own broad claims remains a separate sufficiency inquiry. Neither enumerating targets nor stating intended defensive effects substitutes for a supported teaching across their physical scope. [USPTO MPEP §2164](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2164.html).

A PCT procedure does not prescribe one substantive patentability outcome worldwide. National authorities apply their own substantive law, including prior-art conditions. [PCT Article 27(5)](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/articles/a27). Version-specific public access and any actual filing/priority dates remain distinct; no receipt or accepted filing is evidenced here.

## Contrary and unrelated properties

A later discovery of a previously unrecognized property does not necessarily make an already disclosed identical material novel. Inherent anticipation requires the feature to be necessarily present; merely saying it might occur is insufficient. [USPTO MPEP §2112](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2112.html). Thus an unexpected-property assertion is not automatically a way around an otherwise anticipatory disclosure, and boilerplate naming all outcomes is not proof of inherency.

Theoretical alternatives can be recorded responsibly. Define the material's structure and state, baseline, intervention, observable, conditions, proposed mechanism and domain. Distinguish increased, decreased, unchanged and otherwise altered responses as separate hypotheses, with their supporting sources or theory and explicit uncertainty. Keep ESTABLISHED, CALCULATED, PROPOSED and UNSUPPORTED separate. With no relevant support, an opposite-effect statement remains UNSUPPORTED rather than a prediction of a successful result.

For example, a hypothetical shear-strength comparison and a hypothetical conductivity comparison concern different observables. Describing both signs of each comparison does not establish either magnitude, preparation or use. One fixed specimen and comparison cannot be assigned contradictory observed changes by wording alone. No physical test is scheduled to resolve these hypothetical alternatives under the confirmed scope.

## Tests against illustrative later claims

These are claim-analysis scenarios, not performed physical experiments or legal determinations.

| Later claim scenario | What the package can supply | Gap and possible argument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A specifically taught composition with a supported preparation route | Particular attributed teachings and their actual source boundaries | A claim-specific anticipation argument may be available if every limitation and the relevant prior-art conditions match. General enumeration alone is insufficient. |
| The same elements at selected proportions or in a distinct structure | Mathematical membership and bounds | Neither membership nor element identity establishes the particular phase, connectivity or process. Range specificity and any obviousness rationale need separate assessment. |
| A composition selected from the enormous menu | A way to address its elemental coordinates | Addressability is not a determination that the selected physical species was individually taught and enabled. No automatic novelty conclusion follows. |
| A later process or use, including an unexpected property | Conditional inquiries and any actual earlier relevant teaching | Unprovided process/use limitations remain gaps. Mere possible property changes do not establish necessity or a justified modification; an identical old material's inherent property remains a separate inquiry. |

## Implementation decision

Retain the unrestricted mathematical architecture instead of inventing a metrological floor or replacing it with a finite grid. Preserve supported literature teachings as attributed accounts. Treat unsupported property alternatives as hypotheses, and keep the universal legal result unresolved. More range labels or generic opposite-effect sentences would enlarge wording without supplying the missing physical teaching.

Do not request physical work as a planned next step under the confirmed scope. Further authorized drafting can clarify definitions, sources, current application contents and filing procedure; those changes must be reported for their actual effect. They cannot honestly be described as achieving the universal preemption objective from the present evidence.
