This brief examines all 199 preserved candidate claims in the [application draft](provisional_application_draft.md), with source text checked against repository revision `de3d10fa389a381cb259133e27ee3c099c50a407`. It identifies actual scope, evidence gaps and proposed clarifications. ## Questions requiring different legal analyses

| Inquiry | Required comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Application support under section 112(a) | Assess possession and teaching how to make and use the claimed scope. Written description and enablement are separate requirements. A textual claim-support location is not a determination of either. |
| This disclosure used under section 102 | Compare a particular later claim with a qualifying reference: every limitation must appear in the required combination, expressly or necessarily inherently, and the relied-upon subject matter must be enabled. |
| This disclosure used under section 103 | Identify actual teachings, remaining differences, a supported reason to reach the particular combination and the reasonable expectation of success. Generic inclusion alone does not decide obviousness. |

The first inquiry is governed by the application's actual claim scope. Representative species, identifying characteristics and structure-function relationships can matter to possession of a genus. [MPEP section 2163, II.A.3(a), subsection ii](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2163.html). *Amgen v. Sanofi* requires enablement commensurate with that scope; a reliably enabling common quality or general rule and reasonable experimentation may suffice. It does not require a performed experiment for every embodiment. [Supreme Court opinion, slip pages 13–15](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-757_k5g1.pdf).

For anticipation, a specifically named species may be disclosed even in a long list. Generic membership is a different question. Limited, delineated alternatives can sometimes disclose an immediately envisaged combination, but cannot supply an absent limitation. A disclosed point within a claimed range and a broad overlapping range require different factual comparisons. [MPEP sections 2131, 2131.02 II–III and 2131.03 I–III](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html). Enablement of the particular relied-upon subject matter can draw on skilled knowledge and evidence of known preparation; the author's own synthesis is not categorically required. A non-enabling reference can still contribute what it actually teaches to obviousness. [MPEP sections 2121.01–2121.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html).

Generic species membership alone does not establish obviousness. Overlapping or closely approaching ranges can support a rebuttable prima facie case; the particular teachings, predictability, criticality and rebuttal evidence remain relevant. [MPEP sections 2144.05 and 2144.08](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2144.html). A combination theory also needs a supported rationale and reasonable expectation of success. [MPEP sections 2143 and 2143.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2143.html). Useful teachings may extend beyond what a reference claims. [MPEP section 2123](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2123.html). Failure to support this application's entire genus would therefore not establish that all its specific teachings are useless as prior art; useful teachings would not establish entitlement to that entire genus.

Current PCT guidance addresses full scope, representative examples, reasonable experimentation, physical impossibility, and genus/species novelty distinctions. Its examination is preliminary and nonbinding; national criteria can differ. [ISPE/14, in force 1 January 2026, paragraphs 4.12–4.13, 5.45–5.58 and 12.01–12.10](https://www.wipo.int/documents/d/pct-system/docs-en-texts-ispe-14.pdf), [PCT Article 33(1) and (5)](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/articles/a33).

## Claim families and inherited scope

There are four independent claims: **1, 139, 186 and 194**. Claims 1–185 describe physical materials. Of these, claims 2–138 and 140–185 ultimately depend on claim 1; claim 139 is independent and unary. Claims 186–193 and 197–199 are candidate-material record methods. Claims 194–196 are particle-system record methods. This accounts for **185 + 11 + 3 = 199** claims.

Claim 1 specifies positive normalized atomic or mass fractions and zero fraction for elemental constituents outside a closed inventory. Those express limitations remain relevant throughout its dependency family. The presence of “comprising” does not justify ignoring the exclusions; their construction in the description remains a counsel question. [MPEP section 2111.03](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2111.html). A nominal formula, principal-element ratio, feed mixture, host phase or filler loading must not silently become the claimed whole-material inventory. Claim 139 separately requires atomic fraction one and zero for all other elemental constituents.

The tables form a disjoint partition of the actual claims. Ranges include both endpoints. Each row states additional constraints and the evidence gap; **every physical dependent row retains its parent inventory limitations**. These are technical review findings, not rejection or allowance decisions.

## Clarifications for a subsequent draft

**Isotope fractions in claims 169–173.** The description at D[0047] supports multiplying elemental atomic fraction by a conditional atom-count isotope fraction. Claim 169 also inherits claim 1's mass alternative. Let p_i,a,s be conditional atom-count fractions, mu_i,a,s the qualified isotope/state masses and M_i=sum(p_i,a,s mu_i,a,s). If w_i is the elemental mass fraction, the corresponding nuclide mass fraction is w_i p_i,a,s mu_i,a,s / M_i under that allocation convention. Its atomic fraction instead uses a_i p_i,a,s, where a_i=(w_i/M_i)/sum_j(w_j/M_j). Multiplying w_i by an atom-count conditional fraction alone generally gives neither result. These are CALCULATED accounting distinctions under the stated mass convention, not measurements. Specify whether the intended conditional distribution is atomic or mass-based before revising wording; leave unknown masses unresolved.

**Witness versus target in claims 191–193.** Claim 191 permits a nonnegative numerical witness, while parent claim 186 assigns positive target fractions. For L=(0,1) and U=(0,1), the nonnegative normalized witness (0,1) exists but a positive two-constituent target does not. Clarify a feasibility result's role and reject it as a positive target; claim 192 supplies further positive-support conditions. Do not claim that every implementation uses its exact epsilon construction merely because another exact positive-allocation procedure passes numerical tests.

**Implemented numerical records versus the complete schema.** `composition_record()` and the unrestricted `record()` implement support, target fractions, basis and selected classification/status fields. They do not by themselves implement or populate all structural, preparation, measurement and property fields or each field's evidence status recited in claim 186. The fuller schema at D[0007] also addresses failure and source records; claim 199 adds sources and conditions. A complete worked record may contain explicit unresolved fields; it must not invent their values. Demonstrating those operations would still be different from demonstrating physical manufacture.

**Property scenarios in claims 197–199.** These are dependent record methods: 197 and 199 depend on 186; 198 depends on 197. Recording alternative signs does not show that a physical material necessarily exhibits each response. Inherency needs necessity supported by facts or technical reasoning, rather than a possible outcome. [MPEP sections 2112 I–IV and 2112.01 II](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2112.html). Evidence of unexpected results requires appropriate comparisons, connection to the claimed invention and scope support; a list of contrary predictions is not that evidence. [MPEP sections 716.01(b)–(c) and 716.02(b), (d) and (e)](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s716.html). Keep prophetic work identifiable as unperformed. [MPEP section 2164.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2164.html).

## Evidence needed for the next substantive application version

The next version should connect actual applicant contributions to specific supported embodiments and explain any justified generalization. Preserve the broad requested catalog as an organizing scope while recording which teachings support which limitations. For each selected subject matter, supply the actual contribution and contributors, composition basis and specimen boundary, preparation or technically justified route, structure/state, confirmation and uncertainty, property mechanism or test, failure boundaries and supporting source dates. Working experiments are not universally required; unsupported fields cannot be filled by assertion.

Then assess an actual claim or later-claim comparison limitation by limitation. Record express disclosure, necessary inherency, known preparation evidence, remaining differences, selection rationale, expected success and any contrary evidence. An old attributed teaching remains an old teaching; repackaging it does not create a new applicant contribution. New processes, uses, structures or measured ranges require their own comparisons. No percentage of future patents blocked is assigned.
