Section 102 requires a qualifying enabling reference disclosing every limitation as arranged, expressly or through supported inherency. An enabled species can anticipate a genus; that direction does not automatically reverse, and matching vocabulary is unnecessary. [MPEP §2131-§2131.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html). Inherency requires necessity, not a possibility; skilled knowledge may help establish reference enablement without converting an absent limitation into an express teaching. [MPEP §2112](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2112.html), [§2121](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html). Section 103 requires the actual difference, supporting teaching, articulated rationale and reasonable expectation of success. Unexamined routine-characterization or routine-recording assertions are insufficient. [MPEP §2143](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2143.html).
