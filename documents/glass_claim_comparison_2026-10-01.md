## Governing comparison rules

For §102, a qualifying reference must supply the arrangement of every actual limitation, expressly or inherently. A species can defeat a broader genus; the reverse needs a specifically disclosed or sufficiently bounded, immediately envisaged species. A disclosed point within a claimed range and a broad overlapping range pose different specificity questions. [MPEP §§2131, 2131.02(I)–(III), 2131.03(I)–(III)](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2131.html).

Inherency requires necessity, not possibility. A justified prima facie identity showing can affect the evidentiary burden; an unknown does not establish a distinguishing feature. Nominal ratios alone do not identify the retained composition, structure and history needed for added physical limitations. [MPEP §2112 and §2112.01](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2112.html).

Reference enablement considers the disclosure with skilled knowledge. During examination, relevant publications can carry an operability/enablement presumption subject to appropriate challenge. The author need not have performed every embodiment or printed every routine step. This differs from support for the full scope of the applicant's own claims. [MPEP §2121(I)–(III), §2121.01 and §2121.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2121.html).

For §103, identify the relevant art, differences, supported modification/combination rationale and reasonable expectation of success, and assess relevant contrary evidence. A broad genus-based species argument depends on the actual disclosure and selection reasons, not membership alone. [MPEP §2143(I), §2143.01 and §2143.02(I)](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2143.html), [§2144.08(I)–(II)](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2144.html).

Information content is not automatically a physical difference. Nor may a limitation simply be disregarded because it concerns information: determine whether it is descriptive material and whether a functional relationship gives it patentable weight. Normalization/witness operations and informational labels require separate analysis. [MPEP §2111.05, introduction and I](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2111.html).

These are U.S. rules; other jurisdictions need separate review. Recorded source dates do not decide prior-art qualification for an unidentified application or later claim. Current preparation dates and repository versions do not backdate current curation into the earlier article. No inventor identity, contribution, exception, filing or effective filing date is assumed.

## Complete support, principal target and present record

Three objects remain distinct: the source-associated retained specimen with its actual support; the application's ideal principal-only target/count model; and current record construction assigning that target while recording material and evidence fields. Their denominators and outcome statuses cannot be exchanged.

For an expressly hypothetical ordinary finite specimen consisting entirely of recognized elemental constituents and containing at least two distinct elements, select S to contain every element actually present. Positive counts give x_i=N_i/N and sum(x_i)=1; every label outside complete S has zero count by definition. This addresses the variable-support branch of claim 1 and atom-count restrictions in 144 and 148. Reducing the denominator addresses 149. The complete support need not equal the nominal principal list. This is a construction consequence, not an assertion of an exhaustive source assay. An enabled qualifying prior species satisfying this construction could supply a broad-claim §102 argument, subject to chronology and construction of the informational phrases.

Claim 6 separately requires exactly six positive constituents in the claimed denominator. The principal-only subtotal supports a six-coordinate model; additional retained constituents may change the complete support. Conversely, a possible impurity is not established merely because it could exist. Uncertainty neither proves claim 6 nor automatically excludes claim 1.

Where the expressly identified basis specifies how an existing composition is described, the source convention and skilled understanding may matter without a new exhaustive assay. If construction requires an additional physically operative specification or record relationship, identify that requirement and evidence. Do not import claim 186's operations into independent material claim 1.

Finite atom-count rationality does not entail membership in a fixed decimal grid. The printed target's grid/count compatibility is current CALCULATED accounting, not a retrospective source assay. Mass fractions require their own allocation and do not automatically inherit the arithmetic properties of atom-count fractions. No specimen size, atom network or isotope population follows from the count model.
